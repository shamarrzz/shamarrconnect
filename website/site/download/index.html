How it works

1
Download and run

Open the installer. It is already set up, with nothing to configure.

2
Get your 9-digit ID

ShamarrConnect assigns your device a permanent ID. Share it with anyone you want to give access.

3
Connect

Enter the other device's ID, accept the request, and you're in. Every session is private.
