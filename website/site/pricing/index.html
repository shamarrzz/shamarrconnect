Pricing FAQs

Common questions about plans and billing.

Do paid plans include support?
Yes. Pro includes email support and Team adds priority support. Enterprise customers get a dedicated account manager and an SLA. The Free plan is community-supported.
Is there a free trial?
The Free plan is the easiest trial: 3 devices, no card required. Paid plans include a 14-day money-back guarantee.
Can I pay monthly, or only annually?
Both. Annual billing saves roughly 20% versus monthly. Use the toggle above to compare. We don't auto-renew without notice. You'll get a reminder before any renewal.
How do I upgrade or downgrade?
You can change plans at any time from your account console. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
Pay in Naira via Paystack: cards, bank transfer, USSD or Apple Pay.Pay in USD with international cards via our payment partner. Bank transfer for annual or Enterprise plans: contact sales.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. The price you see covers everything listed in the plan. No setup fees, no per-session charges.
Do you offer non-profit or education discounts?
Yes. We offer discounts for registered non-profits and educational institutions. Get in touch with a bit about your organization.
Where does my data live, and is it private?
Sessions are locked end-to-end between your devices. Our servers only pass along scrambled data they cannot read. And your account lives on our own systems, not with a third party.
