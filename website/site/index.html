Only the people in it can see what happens, on the wire and in the room.

Your session, your rules

You choose what a partner may do.

View only, keyboard and mouse, file transfer, or a private screen only you can see.

  • On-connect privacy prompt. Blank the remote monitor the moment you connect so nobody in the room can watch.
  • All your screens & Virtual Display. Switch between physical monitors seamlessly, or create a virtual screen even on a headless PC.
  • Live permissions & audit trail. Toggle control at any time and review which screen mode was used in your account history.
